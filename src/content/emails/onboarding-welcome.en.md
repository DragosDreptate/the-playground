Thanks for joining The Playground! I'd like to tell you what's behind this project, because you're now part of it.

You've probably been part of communities that mattered to you. Behind each one, there's a person or a team keeping it alive. And they're often fighting tools that work against them. Meetup charges them for access to their own community, and holds their audience hostage if they want to leave. Luma makes beautiful pages, but forgets everything from one event to the next.

Running a community is useful, generous work. It deserves a tool that lives up to it: free, built in Europe, open source, where every community belongs to the people who bring it to life.

That's The Playground. Nothing more, nothing less.

## Where we are

The platform is up and running. The foundations are solid, the features are there.

The first communities, in France and beyond, have moved in, and that's already a sign of trust and usefulness. The Playground gets better every week, and it's built to last.

But for a community platform to prove something, it takes more. More communities that are alive, that grow, whose members keep coming back.

You created an account. That means something resonated. And that matters.

## What I'd like to ask you

Not a like. Not a share (though I wouldn't say no).

Just this: if you know someone who runs events regularly (a meetup, a workshop, a professional network, casual get-togethers, anything really), tell them about The Playground.

> One person. One conversation.

That's how tools that last get built.
